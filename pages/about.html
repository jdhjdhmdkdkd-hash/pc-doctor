About Us

PC Doctor is a technology-focused platform designed to help users understand their computer's performance and hardware.

Our goal is to provide simple and useful tools for checking PC health, hardware information, performance estimates and common computer problems.

What We Provide

  • PC Health Scanner
  • Hardware Information
  • CPU, RAM, GPU and Storage analysis
  • FPS Calculator
  • Bottleneck Checker
  • PC Troubleshooting information
  • Helpful PC guides and articles

PC Doctor is designed to make PC information easier to understand, especially for users who are not technical experts.
